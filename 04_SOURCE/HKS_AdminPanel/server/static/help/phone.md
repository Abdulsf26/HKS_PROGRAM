# Open the panel from your phone

- Phone and laptop must be on the SAME Wi-Fi.
- On the laptop, run CHECK_STATUS.bat - it prints the address to type into the phone browser, e.g. http://192.168.1.50:8000
- Certificate warning (https)? NORMAL - tap Advanced / Proceed.
- Away from home? Use the tunnel shortcut (HKS Phone Access) or HKS_FIX_TUNNEL.bat for a fresh link.
- Away from home and the tunnel address will not open? Office/school networks (FortiGuard, FortiGate, Zscaler) often block tunnel addresses. Open it from mobile data instead - or use the LAN address on your own Wi-Fi.
